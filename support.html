NewsWire Support

Help with the NewsWire news reader for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Common questions

How do I add a feed?

Tap + above the article list, or Add Feed at the bottom of the sidebar. Type a website's name to search for its feed, or paste a website, RSS or Atom address and tap Add.

A site I want doesn't show up in search.

Try pasting the site's web address instead, such as example.com. NewsWire looks for the feed on the site itself. A few sites don't publish a feed at all, so they can't be added.

How do I rename, reorder or remove a feed?

In the sidebar, swipe left on a feed for Rename and Delete, or touch and hold it for more options. To reorder, touch and hold a feed and drag it.

A feed shows a warning triangle.

NewsWire couldn't update that feed the last time it tried. This is usually temporary; pull down on the article list to try again. If it keeps happening, the site may have moved its feed, so remove it and add it again.

How do I see the original page instead of reader mode?

Open an article and tap the page button at the top. NewsWire remembers your choice for later articles. You can also change it in Settings → Open Articles in Reader.

A website looks broken, or I want to support it with ads.

Open one of its articles, tap More (…) and choose Allow Ads on This Site. You can turn ad blocking off for every site in Settings.

My feeds aren't syncing to my other devices.

Sync uses your iCloud account. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and open NewsWire on both. Changes usually arrive within a minute. Articles and read status stay on each device; only your feed list syncs.

Where are Settings?

Tap the … menu above the article list, or the gear at the bottom of the sidebar. With a keyboard, press ⌘, (Command-comma).
