NewsWire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

NewsWire does not collect any personal data. The developer, Neil Lofts, runs no servers for the app and never receives information about you or how you use it.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No data is sold or shared with data brokers.

The rest of this policy explains what stays on your device, what goes to your own iCloud account, and which outside services the app connects to when you use it. It applies to the NewsWire app for iPhone and iPad.

Information stored on your device

NewsWire keeps the following only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's private storage. The developer cannot see it.

WhatWhy
The feeds you subscribe to (address, name, order)To show and refresh your feeds
Downloaded articles: headlines, summaries, links, imagesTo show articles quickly and offline
Which articles you have readTo show unread counts and the Unread list
Settings: appearance, reader mode, mark read on scroll, ad blocking and sites you allowed ads onTo remember your choices
Cookies and site data from pages opened in their original layoutSet by those websites so their pages work; see below

Pages shown in reader mode are loaded without saving cookies or site data.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, NewsWire stores your list of subscribed feeds (each feed's address, name and position) in your own iCloud account, so it appears on your other devices. Articles, read status and settings are not synced.

This data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud account or its contents. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for NewsWire in Settings > [your name] > iCloud where it is listed, or sign out of iCloud. The app works the same without it.

Websites and feeds you add

NewsWire connects directly to the websites whose feeds you add, to download their feeds, article images and pages. Like any web browser, those requests show the website your device's IP address and identify the app as "NewsWire". The developer is not involved in these connections and receives nothing from them.

When you open an article in its original layout, the website's own page loads inside the app. That website may use cookies, its own analytics or ads under its own privacy policy. NewsWire's ad blocking, which is on by default, blocks many known ad and tracking domains, but it cannot block everything. Links you open in Safari or share are handled by Safari or the app you share to.

Feed search

When you type a website's name in Add Feed, NewsWire sends what you typed to Feedly's public feed directory (cloud.feedly.com) to find matching feeds. Feedly receives the search words and your device's IP address. Nothing else is sent: no account, name, email or device identifier.

Feedly handles this under Feedly's Privacy Policy. Typing or pasting a web or feed address instead does not use Feedly; the app goes straight to that site.

Third parties, tracking and advertising

NewsWire contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track you.

The only outside services the app talks to are:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhen
Apple iCloudYour list of subscribed feedsWhenever the list changes, if you use iCloud
FeedlyYour search words and IP addressWhen you search for a site by name
Websites you addYour IP address and normal web requestsWhen feeds refresh or you open an article

These companies handle data under their own privacy policies. The developer has no agreement with them to receive your data, and none is passed back to the developer.

If you choose to share crash reports and app usage with developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash and performance data. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Keeping and deleting your data

You control all of it, and none of it is held by the developer, so there is nothing to request from us.

  • A feed: remove it in the app. Its articles are deleted from your device, and it is removed from iCloud and your other devices. A small removal record stays in your iCloud for up to 90 days so offline devices also remove the feed.
  • Stored articles: each feed keeps at most its 100 most recent articles; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app. This erases its articles, read status, settings and cookies from that device.
  • Your synced feed list: remove your feeds in the app before deleting it, or turn off iCloud for NewsWire. Data in iCloud is otherwise kept and deleted under Apple's terms.

If you stop using iCloud sync or search, you can still use every other part of the app.

Children

NewsWire is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Because the developer does not collect or hold any personal data, there is none to provide; you can manage everything yourself as described above. You can contact us with any question about your rights.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top changed. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
